MacBook DA Sync 是应用所有者自用的 Mac 桌面工具，定时把选定的经营与技术报告复制到其 Google Drive 的 MacBook 文件夹。

A personal Mac utility that periodically copies selected business and technical reports to its owner's MacBook folder in Google Drive. It is not a public sign-up service.

它如何工作

工具在所有者的 Mac 本机运行，通过 rclone 和 macOS 原生定时器执行复制，并核对文件名称、大小和校验值。原有报告更新后，云端对应副本随之更新；本地删除不会自动删除云端副本。

The utility runs locally using rclone and the native macOS scheduler. It copies updated report documents and verifies their names, sizes and checksums. Local deletions are not propagated to Drive.

处理的内容

所有者选择的报告文件，以及完成和核验传输所需的文件名、大小、校验值与运行时间。

Selected report documents and the file metadata required to transfer and verify them.

排除的内容

报告原件目录、原始录制流、凭据、私钥、符号链接和超过上限的文件均被筛除。这个说明站不展示任何报告内容。

Originals, raw recording streams, credentials, private keys, symbolic links and oversized files are excluded. This information website displays no report contents.

为什么请求 Google Drive 权限

Google OAuth 的 Drive 权限用于创建、更新和核验所选报告的云端副本。Google 显示的权限范围允许查看、修改、创建和删除 Drive 文件；本工具的执行逻辑只在配置的 MacBook 目录内复制与核验，不运行删除或跨目录同步操作。

固定目标目录是工具的操作限制，不等于 Google 在服务端把 OAuth 权限缩小到该目录。

The Drive OAuth scope permits viewing, editing, creating and deleting Drive files. This utility uses copy and verification operations within its configured MacBook destination, without deletion or cross-folder synchronization. The configured destination is an operational restriction, not a server-side OAuth permission boundary.

数据由所有者控制

授权信息保存在上传源之外的本机私密配置中。所有者可停止定时任务，并在 Google 账号中撤销授权；已经存在的云端副本由所有者另行管理。

Authorization information is stored in private local configuration outside the upload source. The owner can stop the scheduler and revoke the app's Google authorization. Existing cloud copies remain under the owner's control.
